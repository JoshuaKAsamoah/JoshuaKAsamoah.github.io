we developed SAAM-ReflectNet, a deep learning framework that unifies traffic sign detection, classification, and retroreflectivity estimation into a single automated pipeline. Our RetroNet backbone, developed as part of this study, extracts robust spatial and semantic features to enhance feature representation. The Sign-Aware Attention Module we designed prioritizes critical traffic sign regions, improving detection and classification accuracy by focusing on the most relevant areas.
